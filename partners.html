Down.Town readers are researching a specific downtown, running the numbers in our tools, and then asking for help. Join the partner network to receive their requests — reviewed by a person, consented, and shared with no more than three providers.

Who we work with

Tenant-side commercial brokers

Office, retail and industrial-flex requirements with headcount, size, lease length and timing.

Coworking and flexible office operators

Desk, private office and managed-suite requests, usually with a start date within three months.

Event venues and planners

Guest count, layout, date, catering and budget — from boardroom offsites to galas.

Rental agents and apartment locators

Bedrooms, budget, move-in month, household and must-haves for downtown rentals.

Realtors and mortgage advisers

Purchase budget, pre-approval status, bedrooms and timeline for condos and townhomes.

Movers and relocation firms

Household and office moves, team relocations, temporary housing and storage.

What a request looks like

Each introduction arrives as a structured brief, so you can qualify it in seconds.

RequestWhat you receive
OfficeCity, preferred area, headcount, work pattern, size estimate, lease length, timing, must-haves, company
Retail / restaurantUse type, size range, budget, frontage and kitchen needs, opening date
CoworkingDesks, type (private office, dedicated, hot desk), term, start date
VenueEvent type, guests, layout, date and flexibility, catering, budget
Rent / buyBedrooms, budget, household, pre-approval, move-in timing, must-haves
RelocationTeam size, origin city, services needed, timeline

Every brief includes the person's preferred contact method and consent record.

Ways to partner

Referral share

Pay only when a deal you were introduced to closes: a share of your commission or fee, agreed per category. Best for brokers and agents.

Pricing on request
Pay per introduction

A fixed fee for each introduction you accept. Decline anything outside your area or capacity at no cost.

Pricing on request
City Exclusive

One partner per category per downtown, introduced first on every matching request, plus a labelled profile on that city's space or guide page.

Monthly, on request

Our standards

  • Licensed or registered where your profession requires it
  • First response within one business day of an introduction
  • Clear, honest pricing and no pressure selling
  • Use a person's details only for the request they sent — never for unrelated marketing
  • Tell us when an introduction converts so we can keep matching well

We may decline or pause partners who fall short. Partner arrangements never influence editorial coverage, scores or rankings.
