Accessibility

We want Down.Town to work for everyone. The site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

  • Keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators and a skip link.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful graphics; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Colour contrast checked in light and dark themes; information is never conveyed by colour alone.
  • Reduced motion respected for visitors who prefer it.
  • Forms with labels, clear errors and status messages announced to screen readers.

If you find a barrier, please tell us — include the page and what happened, and we'll respond within five business days.
