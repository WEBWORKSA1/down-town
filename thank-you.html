What happens next

  1. We review it within one business day

    An editor reads every submission by hand. Nothing is forwarded automatically.

  2. We act on it

    For introductions, we pick up to three partners who fit and send the introductions by email. For listings, we check the details and send pricing if you chose a paid tier. For questions and enquiries, we reply, usually within two business days.

  3. Watch your inbox

    Replies come by email, so check your spam or promotions folder if you don't see one. If you asked for introductions and a partner hasn't been in touch within 48 hours of being introduced, tell us and we'll chase.

Forgot something?

Send the extra detail through the contact form with the same email address, and we'll add it to your original message.

Your details
