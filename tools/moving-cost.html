Where downtown moves go over budget

Crew hours are the swing factor. In a tower, movers may wait for a service elevator, work within a booked window and carry from a loading dock rather than the front door. Each delay is billed. Book the elevator and dock early, send your mover the building's rules, and ask whether a parking permit is needed for the truck on the street.

Overlap rent is the other hidden cost. Few leases line up to the day, and paying two rents for a week or two gives you time to clean, repair and hand back the old place properly — which protects your deposit.

Cutting the bill

  • Move mid-month and mid-week, when crews are easier to book.
  • Sell or donate furniture that will not fit a smaller downtown floor plan before you pay to move it.
  • Pack books, kitchenware and decorations yourself; leave fragile and heavy items to the crew.
  • Ask landlords about move-in incentives in buildings with vacancies.
