Good to know

What's depreciation?

The value a car loses each year. Even a paid-off car costs money this way. A rough approach: what you paid minus what it would sell for now, divided by the years you've owned it, then divided by 12.

What if I still need a car sometimes?

That's what the rental and ride-hail lines are for. Most car-free households still use cars — they just pay only when they use one.
