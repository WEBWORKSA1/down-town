Good to know

Where do I get foot-traffic numbers?

Count them. Stand across the street at the hours you'd trade and count people passing for 15-minute blocks, then scale up. Some landlords and business associations also share pedestrian counts.

What's a healthy occupancy ratio?

Many retail operators aim to keep total occupancy costs to roughly 10% of sales or less, and many restaurants to a similar range. It varies by format and margin — treat it as a sanity check, not a rule.

What entry and purchase rates should I use?

Use your own counts from a similar shop if you can. Otherwise run a range: low, middle and high. The point is to see whether the space works even on a cautious estimate.
