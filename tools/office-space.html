How the calculator works

The calculator starts from your planning headcount: today's team plus the growth you expect during the lease. It multiplies that by a per-person allowance that depends on how you work. A team that is in the office every day needs a desk each and roughly 150–200 square feet per person once meeting rooms, kitchen and circulation are included. A hybrid team that shares desks on a 70% ratio needs closer to 100–150. A team that is mostly remote and uses the office as a hub for meetings and collaboration days can work with 60–100.

Those figures give usable area, the space inside your demised premises. Landlords in most multi-tenant buildings charge on rentable area, which adds your share of lobbies, corridors and washrooms. The load factor field adds that share; 10–20% is common, but ask for the building's measured figure because it varies widely between older and newer towers.

Meeting rooms and focus space

Teams under-provide meeting space more than anything else. A workable starting point is one bookable meeting room for every 10–15 people and one phone or focus room for every eight, adjusted for how much of your work is video calls. Client-facing firms usually need a larger room near reception; engineering teams often prefer more small rooms and quiet zones.

From a number to a brief

Take the range, not the midpoint, to brokers and landlords. Ask each to provide a test fit — a quick layout of your requirements on their floor plate — because column grids, window lines and core position change how efficiently a given area works. Two floors with the same rentable area can house very different headcounts.

When you add rent and operating costs, the calculator multiplies them by the rentable area at the middle of your range to give an annual occupancy figure. It ignores fit-out, furniture, IT, moving and incentives such as rent-free months, which can be large; see negotiating office rent and the office relocation timeline for those.

Small team?

Below roughly 15 people, coworking private offices and fitted suites often win on cost and flexibility. Check your break-even with the coworking vs lease tool.
