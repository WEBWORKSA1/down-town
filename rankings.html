How the Index works

Each downtown receives an editorial score from 1 to 10 in eight categories. Scores are assigned by Down.Town editors after reviewing transit maps and service patterns, the density of amenities within walking distance of the core, cultural institutions, the business environment and the relative cost of housing and daily life. They are judgements, not official statistics, and they compare city centres with each other rather than with an absolute standard.

The overall Index is a weighted average, scaled to 100. Weights: walk ×1.2, transit ×1.2, food ×1.0, night ×0.8, culture ×1.0, family ×0.8, business ×1.0, value ×1.0. Walkability and transit carry slightly more weight because they shape daily life in a downtown more than any other factor.

We review scores at least once a year and whenever a major change — a new transit line, a large rezoning, a sharp change in housing costs — shifts the picture. Disagree with a score? Tell us why; good evidence from readers can change a score.
