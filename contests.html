How it works

  1. Shoot

    Photograph any downtown or city centre in the world. Photos must be your own and taken within the last two years.

  2. Submit

    Send a share link (Google Drive, Dropbox, Flickr or your portfolio) with the form below, by February 28, 2027.

  3. Judging

    Editors shortlist entries in March 2027, judging composition, originality, technical quality and sense of place.

  4. Community vote

    Readers vote for Community Choice from the shortlist.

  5. Winners

    Announced in April 2027 on this page and in the Downtown Dispatch.

Enter Season 1

JPEG, at least 2000 px on the long edge. Make sure the link is viewable by anyone.

Best Block Awards

Know an independent downtown business that makes its block better? Nominate it. Winners get a feature in their city guide, a winner's badge for their window and website, and a free year of Spotlight listing.

Official rules (summary)

  • Organiser: Down.Town (down.town). Contact via the contact form.
  • No purchase or payment necessary. Entering is free. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  • Eligibility: open to individuals 18 or older, worldwide, except where prohibited. Down.Town staff, judges and their immediate families may not win prizes.
  • Entry period: December 1, 2026, 00:00 UTC to February 28, 2027, 23:59 UTC. Entries outside the period are not eligible.
  • Entries: up to five photographs per person across all categories, taken in a downtown or city centre within two years before submission. No AI-generated or composited images; standard editing is fine. Entrants must hold all rights and any required permissions from identifiable people or property owners.
  • Judging: category and grand-prize winners are chosen on merit — composition, originality, technical quality and sense of place — by Down.Town editors and any category sponsor judges. Community Choice is decided by a public vote among shortlisted entries; one vote per person, and suspicious voting may be disqualified.
  • Prizes: Grand Prize US$500 plus a cover feature; five category prizes of US$100; Community Choice US$100. One prize per person. Cash prizes are paid electronically within 30 days of winner verification; winners are responsible for any taxes. Sponsor-provided prizes may be added and will be described on this page.
  • Winner verification: winners will be contacted by email and must respond within 14 days and confirm eligibility and rights, or an alternate may be selected.
  • Licence: entrants keep copyright and grant Down.Town a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to display, reproduce and share entries, with credit, for the contest and its promotion. No other commercial use without separate agreement.
  • Privacy: entry data is used to run the contest as described in our privacy policy.
  • General: Down.Town may cancel, suspend or modify the contest if it cannot run as planned, and may disqualify entries that break these rules. Decisions of the judges are final.
