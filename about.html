About Down.Town

An independent guide to the places where cities come together.

Downtowns are where cities concentrate everything: work, culture, transit, history, nightlife and, increasingly, homes. They are also changing faster than at any time in decades, as offices become apartments, streets become parks and storefronts find new uses. Down.Town exists to help people make good decisions about them.

What we publish

  • City guides to 26 downtowns, all built on the same structure so you can compare them fairly: districts, landmarks, food, getting around, living, doing business, events and scores.
  • The Down.Town Index, an editorial ranking on eight measures of downtown life.
  • Practical guides for people moving downtown and for owners opening or running a business there.
  • Free tools — a city match quiz, comparisons, a rent budget calculator, a car-versus-car-free calculator and a storefront revenue estimator.

How we make money

We believe readers should know how a publication is paid for. Down.Town earns revenue from:

  • Display advertising served by Google AdSense.
  • Labelled sponsorships, such as City Partner placements and newsletter sponsors.
  • Paid business listings (Featured and Spotlight), always marked as such.
  • Referral fees from partners when readers ask us for introductions — only with the reader's explicit consent.
  • Reader support.

None of these buy editorial verdicts. Sponsors cannot change Index scores, and paid placements never appear as editorial recommendations.

Editorial standards

Every guide is researched against primary sources — official city, transit and tourism information — and reviewed at least once a year. Read our full editorial policy, including how we use technology tools and how we handle corrections.

Independence

Down.Town is not affiliated with any city government, business improvement district, downtown association, developer or organisation whose name includes the word "downtown". See our trademark and copyright notice.
