Review standards

What we check before a listing goes live

  • Real, open businesses only. An operating business with a physical location in or near one of our downtowns, or a service that genuinely operates there. No virtual addresses or placeholder pages.
  • Accurate details. We check your name, address and website against your own site, public records or a quick call.
  • Honest descriptions. Your own words, without claims you can't back up, fake reviews or ratings, or prices that will soon be out of date.
  • Lawful and licensed. We decline businesses operating without a required licence, and content that would break the law or Google's publisher policies.
  • One listing per location. Chains and franchises can list each downtown location separately.

We may decline any listing, and we remove listings for businesses that close or whose details stop being accurate. Paid tiers don't change these standards.

How it works

From form to live listing

  1. Submit

    Send the form below. It takes a couple of minutes.

  2. An editor checks it

    We pick up new submissions within one business day. Verification can take a little longer if we need to confirm a detail with you.

  3. You approve

    For Featured or Spotlight, we send pricing and the placement for you to agree in writing. Basic listings skip this step.

  4. It goes live

    Paid placements are labelled "Featured" or "Spotlight", so readers always know what is paid.

  5. Keep it current

    Moved, renamed or changed your hours? Tell us through the contact form.

What paid listings don't buy

  • Editorial coverage or a recommendation in a guide
  • A better Down.Town Index score for your city
  • A place in Get matched introductions, which go to partners chosen on fit
  • Changes to anything our editors write
