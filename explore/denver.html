Downtown Denver

A restored railway station, brick warehouse districts and Rocky Mountain views from a mile-high downtown.

Denver's downtown sits on a grid at the meeting point of the South Platte River and Cherry Creek, with the Rocky Mountains on the western horizon. Lower Downtown, known as LoDo, is the historic heart: brick warehouses turned into restaurants, lofts and galleries around a beautifully restored Union Station.

The 16th Street corridor connects LoDo with the Civic Center and the cultural district, while the River North Art District just to the north has become one of the city's most creative neighbourhoods.

Landmarks worth your time

  • Union Station. A restored 1914 rail hall that is now a hotel, bar and transport hub.
  • Larimer Square. The city's oldest block, strung with lights above restaurants and shops.
  • Coors Field. The baseball stadium on the edge of LoDo.
  • Denver Art Museum. A major art museum with an angular titanium-clad wing.
  • Colorado State Capitol. A gold-domed capitol with a step marking an elevation of one mile.
  • Confluence Park. A riverside park where the creek meets the river, popular for summer tubing.

Eat and drink

Denver's dining scene has grown fast, with chef-driven restaurants in LoDo and RiNo, and craft breweries everywhere. Mexican and Southwestern flavours are local strengths.

Getting around

RTD runs light rail and commuter rail; the A Line connects Union Station with the airport. Downtown is walkable, and bike lanes and scooters link the surrounding neighbourhoods. Check RTD for current downtown shuttle service along the 16th Street corridor.

Living downtown

LoDo lofts and new apartments near Union Station appeal to young professionals. Housing is cheaper than coastal cities but has risen steeply over the past decade.

Doing business downtown

Energy, aerospace, telecom, outdoor-industry brands and tech fill downtown offices. Retail and hospitality depend heavily on sports crowds and conventions at the Colorado Convention Center.

  • Start at Union Station and walk to Larimer Square.
  • Day-trip to the mountains early to avoid traffic.

Questions people ask

What is the best area to stay in downtown Denver?

It depends on your plans. LoDo is the most central choice: historic warehouses, Union Station and Larimer Square. For a different feel, consider 16th Street corridor — the pedestrian-focused spine of downtown.

How do you get around downtown Denver?

RTD runs light rail and commuter rail; the A Line connects Union Station with the airport. Walking is the best way to explore the core itself.

When is the best time to visit Denver?

Down.Town's pick is June–September. Signature events include Great American Beer Festival (Autumn).

Is downtown Denver a good place to live?

LoDo lofts and new apartments near Union Station appeal to young professionals. It scores 8/10 for walkability, 7/10 for transit and 5/10 for value in the Down.Town Index.
