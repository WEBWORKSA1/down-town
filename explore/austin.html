Downtown Austin

A live-music capital with a state capitol, a lake trail and a bridge full of bats.

Downtown Austin has grown upward fast, with new towers rising around Congress Avenue and Rainey Street. Yet the street level still feels like the city's identity: live music venues on Red River Street, bars on Sixth Street and the green ribbon of Lady Bird Lake on the southern edge.

The Texas State Capitol closes the north end of Congress Avenue, and every evening in the warm months a large colony of bats emerges from under the Congress Avenue Bridge — one of the more unusual downtown spectacles anywhere.

Landmarks worth your time

  • Texas State Capitol. A grand granite Capitol with free tours and landscaped grounds.
  • Lady Bird Lake. A reservoir on the Colorado River, ringed by a hike-and-bike trail of about 10 miles.
  • Congress Avenue Bridge bats. A large urban bat colony that emerges at dusk in the warm months.
  • Zilker Park and Barton Springs Pool. A large park and spring-fed swimming pool just southwest of downtown.
  • Paramount Theatre. A historic theatre on Congress Avenue hosting films, comedy and music.
  • Mexic-Arte Museum. A museum of Mexican, Latino and Latin American art on Congress Avenue.

Eat and drink

Austin is known for barbecue and Tex-Mex, with queues forming early at the most famous smokehouses. Food trucks and trailer parks are part of the scene, and the Rainey Street and East Sixth areas mix bars with casual kitchens.

Getting around

CapMetro runs buses and the MetroRail Red Line, which stops downtown. Voters approved the Project Connect transit plan for future rail. Downtown is walkable, and the lake trail serves cyclists and runners, but most of the region is car-dependent.

Living downtown

High-rise condos and apartments have grown quickly downtown, especially around Rainey Street and the Seaholm district. Texas has no state personal income tax, though property taxes are relatively high.

Doing business downtown

Technology companies, the state government, the University of Texas and the music and events economy anchor downtown. Festival weeks bring short, intense surges of visitors and pop-up activations.

Local tips

  • Book hotels early for SXSW and race weekends.
  • Rent a kayak or paddleboard on Lady Bird Lake.
  • Carry water in summer; heat is intense.

Questions people ask

What is the best area to stay in downtown Austin?

It depends on your plans. Congress Avenue is the most central choice: the main street, running from the Capitol to the lake. For a different feel, consider Rainey Street — old bungalows converted into bars, surrounded by new apartment towers.

How do you get around downtown Austin?

CapMetro runs buses and the MetroRail Red Line, which stops downtown. Walking is the best way to explore the core itself.

When is the best time to visit Austin?

Down.Town's pick is March–May, October–November. Signature events include SXSW (March).

Is downtown Austin a good place to live?

High-rise condos and apartments have grown quickly downtown, especially around Rainey Street and the Seaholm district. It scores 7/10 for walkability, 5/10 for transit and 5/10 for value in the Down.Town Index.
