Downtown Cape Town

A downtown cradled by Table Mountain, with a colourful heritage quarter and a working waterfront.

City Bowl, South AfricaDown.Town Index 71/100Reviewed October 2026

Cape Town's City Bowl is a natural amphitheatre formed by Table Mountain, Lion's Head and Signal Hill, opening onto Table Bay. The CBD's grid holds historic buildings, the Company's Garden and busy streets.

Bo-Kaap's colourful houses sit on the slopes above, and the V&A Waterfront is a working harbour transformed into shops, restaurants and museums.

Landmarks worth your time

  • Table Mountain. Reached by a rotating cableway or by hiking trails, with views over the city and the ocean when the weather allows.
  • Company's Garden. A historic public garden in the heart of the city, beside museums and the parliament buildings.
  • Bo-Kaap. Bright houses, mosques and a museum telling the story of the Cape Malay community.
  • Zeitz MOCAA. A museum of contemporary African art inside a dramatically carved former grain silo.
  • Robben Island. The UNESCO-listed island prison where Nelson Mandela was held, reached by ferry from the Waterfront.
  • Castle of Good Hope. A 17th-century star fort, the oldest surviving colonial building in South Africa.

Eat and drink

Cape Malay cooking — bobotie, curries and koeksisters — sits alongside excellent seafood and a fine-dining scene that draws on wine regions an hour away. Bree Street in the CBD is a strip of restaurants and bars, and the Waterfront's food market is good for a casual lunch.

Getting around

MyCiTi bus rapid transit links the CBD with the Waterfront, the Atlantic seaboard and the airport. Ride-hailing is widely used and recommended at night. Minibus taxis are a large part of local transport but can be confusing for newcomers.

Living downtown

City Bowl apartments and houses in Gardens, Tamboerskloof and De Waterkant are popular with professionals. Security is a practical consideration: many buildings have controlled access, and residents tend to drive or use ride-hailing after dark. Power supply has become far more stable in recent years after a long period of scheduled outages.

Doing business downtown

Tourism, film and commercial production, technology startups, financial services and the wine trade drive the economy. Bree Street and the Waterfront attract hospitality businesses, while the tech scene clusters in the CBD and Woodstock. Seasonal tourism peaks in the southern summer, December to March.

Local tips

  • Go up Table Mountain early and check the cableway's status — wind can close it.
  • Book Robben Island ferries ahead, especially in summer.
  • Use ride-hailing at night rather than walking between districts.

Questions people ask

What is the best area to stay in downtown Cape Town?

It depends on your plans. CBD is the most central choice: the grid of historic buildings, Greenmarket Square and the Company's Garden. For a different feel, consider Bo-Kaap — the colourful Cape Malay heritage quarter on the slopes of Signal Hill.

How do you get around downtown Cape Town?

MyCiTi bus rapid transit links the CBD with the Waterfront, the Atlantic seaboard and the airport. Walking is the best way to explore the core itself.

When is the best time to visit Cape Town?

Down.Town's pick is October–April. Signature events include Tweede Nuwe Jaar (January 2).

Is downtown Cape Town a good place to live?

City Bowl apartments and houses in Gardens, Tamboerskloof and De Waterkant are popular with professionals. It scores 7/10 for walkability, 6/10 for transit and 8/10 for value in the Down.Town Index.
