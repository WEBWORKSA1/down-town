Downtown Miami

A bilingual, bayfront financial district with a free elevated train and an art scene to the north.

Downtown Miami and neighbouring Brickell form a forest of residential and office towers on Biscayne Bay. In the last two decades, the area has become a true live-work district, with tens of thousands of residents, rooftop bars and waterfront parks.

The city's Latin American ties shape everything from the language you hear in shops to the banks lining Brickell Avenue. North of downtown, Wynwood's murals and the Design District's boutiques give the city its creative edge.

Landmarks worth your time

  • Pérez Art Museum Miami. A bayfront museum with hanging gardens on its terraces.
  • Bayfront Park. A downtown park that hosts concerts and festivals.
  • Wynwood Walls. An outdoor museum of large-scale murals.
  • Freedom Tower. A historic tower linked to the Cuban exile experience.
  • Frost Museum of Science. A science museum and aquarium in Museum Park.
  • Calle Ocho. The main street of Little Havana.

Eat and drink

Cuban coffee and sandwiches, Peruvian ceviche, Venezuelan arepas and fine-dining rooms in Brickell all compete for attention. Rooftop bars are a big part of the evening scene.

Getting around

The free Metromover loops through downtown and Brickell. Metrorail connects to Miami International Airport, and Brightline trains run from downtown to Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach and Orlando. Citi Bike Miami adds options.

Living downtown

High-rise condos dominate. Florida has no state personal income tax, but insurance costs for property and cars are high and rising. Check hurricane-proofing and flood risk before you sign.

Questions people ask

What is the best area to stay in downtown Miami?

It depends on your plans. Brickell is the most central choice: the financial district of banks, condos and restaurants. For a different feel, consider Downtown and Bayfront — the original core, parks and museums on the bay.

How do you get around downtown Miami?

The free Metromover loops through downtown and Brickell. Walking is the best way to explore the core itself.

When is the best time to visit Miami?

Down.Town's pick is November–April. Signature events include Ultra Music Festival (March).

Is downtown Miami a good place to live?

High-rise condos dominate. It scores 7/10 for walkability, 6/10 for transit and 3/10 for value in the Down.Town Index.
