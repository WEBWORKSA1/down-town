Downtown Paris

Islands, quays and arcades at the historic centre of the city, now reclaimed for walkers and cyclists.

In 2020, Paris merged its first four arrondissements into a single district called Paris Centre. It covers the Louvre, Les Halles, the Marais and the two islands in the Seine — the oldest parts of the city.

This is a downtown that has spent the last decade reducing car traffic. Riverbanks once used by expressways are now promenades, and many streets near the Louvre are reserved for bikes, buses and pedestrians.

Landmarks worth your time

  • The Louvre. A former royal palace and one of the world's great museums.
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral. The Gothic cathedral, reopened in December 2024 after restoration.
  • Sainte-Chapelle. A chapel with soaring stained-glass walls.
  • Place des Vosges. The oldest planned square in Paris, in the Marais.
  • Tuileries Garden. Formal gardens running from the Louvre toward the Place de la Concorde.
  • Seine quays. Riverside promenades turned into public spaces, with summer events.

Eat and drink

Paris Centre is a perfect place to graze. The Marais has bakeries, falafel counters, Jewish delis and natural-wine bars; Rue Montorgueil near Les Halles is a market street of fishmongers, cheese shops and pâtisseries. Classic cafés are for watching the street, not for speed. Many restaurants close between lunch and dinner, and some close in August.

Getting around

The Métro and RER converge at Châtelet–Les Halles. RER B runs to Charles de Gaulle, and Line 14 serves Orly. Vélib' bike share and expanding cycle lanes make biking practical.

Living downtown

Apartments are often in Haussmann-era buildings with small kitchens and lifts that hold two people. Rent control rules set reference rents in Paris. Expect to provide extensive documents for a lease.

Doing business downtown

Luxury retail, fashion, tourism and professional services dominate the centre. The Marais is a hotspot for concept stores and pop-ups, and fashion weeks bring waves of buyers and press. French commercial leases typically run for long terms with renewal rights for the tenant, and taking over an existing lease (cession de bail) is a common way to secure a good storefront.

Local tips

  • Visit the Louvre in the evening on late-opening days.
  • Walk the islands at dawn.
  • Learn a few French phrases; a 'bonjour' goes a long way.

Questions people ask

What is the best area to stay in downtown Paris?

It depends on your plans. Le Marais is the most central choice: medieval streets, mansions, galleries and boutiques. For a different feel, consider Les Halles — the commercial heart above a major rail hub.

How do you get around downtown Paris?

The Métro and RER converge at Châtelet–Les Halles. Walking is the best way to explore the core itself.

When is the best time to visit Paris?

Down.Town's pick is April–June, September–October. Signature events include Fête de la Musique (June 21).

Is downtown Paris a good place to live?

Apartments are often in Haussmann-era buildings with small kitchens and lifts that hold two people. It scores 10/10 for walkability, 10/10 for transit and 3/10 for value in the Down.Town Index.
