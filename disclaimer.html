Disclaimer

Down.Town helps you understand a downtown and prepare for decisions about it. It doesn't replace an official source or a professional who knows your situation. Last updated 29 October 2026.

Guides are general information

Written in good faith from reliable sources, for orientation, not as advice for your circumstances.

Tools give estimates

Calculators use your numbers and rules of thumb. They are not quotes, valuations or forecasts.

Partners are independent

An introduction is not an endorsement of a person, property, price or lease.

Not professional advice

Down.Town publishes general information about downtowns for exploring, living and doing business in them. Nothing on the site is legal, financial, tax, investment, real estate, immigration, medical, safety or travel advice, and reading it, using a tool or contacting us does not create a professional or client relationship. For decisions that matter, such as signing a lease, buying a home, moving a team or opening a business, speak to a qualified professional in the right jurisdiction.

Scores are editorial

Down.Town Index scores are the considered judgement of our editors, made with the published method on the Index page. They are not official statistics, they are not a measure of any one street or building, and they cannot be bought.

Calculators are estimates

Our tools produce estimates from the figures you enter and from general rules of thumb, such as space per person or rent as a share of income. They don't know your finances, the local market, a particular building or the terms on offer. Real costs, sizes and outcomes will differ, sometimes a lot. Use the results to frame questions for a broker, landlord, lender or adviser.

Lease and legal conventions

Our Find Space pages describe how commercial leases and related costs typically work in each country. Conventions vary by city, building and landlord, and laws change. Treat them as orientation, and have a local lawyer review any lease or contract before you sign.

Details change

Prices, opening hours, schedules, transit lines, fares, laws, permits, events and businesses change, often without notice. Event dates move from year to year. We review every guide at least once a year, but something may have changed since. Check anything important with the official source, such as the city, the transit agency or the event organiser, before you travel, book or commit.

Safety and practical notes

Our safety and practicality notes are general, balanced observations to help you plan. They are not guarantees, and conditions can change quickly. Follow official advice from local authorities and your own government's travel guidance.
