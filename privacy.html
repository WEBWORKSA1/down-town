Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

This policy explains what information Down.Town ("we", "us") collects when you use down.town, why, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form — newsletter sign-up, Get Matched request, business listing, contest entry, pledge, application or contact message — we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number (if given) and the content of your request. Forms are delivered to our inbox through a form-processing service (FormSubmit), which acts as our processor for this purpose.

How we use it

  • To respond to you and provide what you asked for (for example, sending the newsletter or reviewing a listing).
  • Partner introductions: if you submit a Get Matched or similar request and tick the consent box, we share your request with up to three relevant partners (such as real-estate agents, movers, commercial brokers or contractors) so they can contact you about it. Partners may pay us a referral fee. We never sell or rent lists of personal information.
  • To run contests as described in the official rules.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

When you first visit, a notice lets you accept all cookies or choose essential only. Choosing essential only requests non-personalised ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may see Google's consent message, which records consent choices for advertising. See our cookie policy for details.

Other services that may receive data

  • Google Fonts delivers our typefaces; your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • YouTube videos load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles load only if you open a map.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose: typically up to 24 months for enquiries and leads, 12 months for job applications, and for the duration of a subscription for the newsletter. Contest records are kept for the contest and any legally required period afterwards.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR (EU/UK), Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25) and US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA — you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your information, to withdraw consent and to object to certain uses. We do not sell personal information in the ordinary sense; if a local law treats partner referrals as a "sale" or "sharing", you can opt out at any time. To exercise any right, use the contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Children

Down.Town is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. Contest entrants must be 18 or older.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top.
