What happens next

  1. We review your request within two business days.
  2. We pick up to three partners who serve your downtown and your budget — agents, rental finders, movers, brokers, lenders or contractors.

Is it free?

Yes. Partners may pay Down.Town a referral fee; that never changes what you pay them.

Who sees my details?

Only Down.Town and the partners we match you with — never more than three, and only because you ticked the consent box. We don't sell lists. Privacy policy.
