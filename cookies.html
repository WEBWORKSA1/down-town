Cookie policy

Last updated October 2026.

Cookies and similar technologies (like local storage) are small pieces of data stored by your browser. Here's what Down.Town uses.

Essential storage (set by us)

NamePurposeDuration
dt-consentRemembers your cookie choiceUntil you clear it
dt-themeRemembers light or dark modeUntil you clear it

Advertising cookies (Google)

Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and — with consent where required — personalise them. See how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Embedded content

YouTube may set cookies after you press play on a video. OpenStreetMap tile servers receive requests if you open a map.

Change your choice

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.
