Our standards

Labelled, separate and fair to readers

  • Always labelled. Every sponsored placement carries a "Sponsored" label, and links in it are marked as sponsored links.
  • No paid editorial. We don't sell articles, reviews, rankings or recommendations. Partners never pay for editorial coverage.
  • Hands off the guides. Sponsors never see or approve editorial content before publication, and can't change a guide or an Index score.
  • Compatible with Google AdSense. Sponsorships are kept separate from Google ads, are never styled to look like them, and are never placed to invite accidental clicks. We never ask anyone to click an ad.
  • Right for readers. We decline sponsors we wouldn't be comfortable putting in front of a reader making a big decision, and anything that would break the law or Google's publisher policies.

The full rules are in our editorial policy. Display advertising on Down.Town is served separately, by Google AdSense.

What happens next

  1. We read your request

    Within one business day, and reply with the media kit and any questions.

    Placements, cities or editions, timing and pricing, in writing.
