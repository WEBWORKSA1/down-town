Before you write

Help us help you faster

Corrections

Tell us the page, what is wrong and where you saw the right information. We check every report against a source and note significant corrections on the page. See our editorial policy.

Privacy requests

Choose "Privacy request" and tell us what you want: a copy of your data, a correction, deletion, or to withdraw consent for introductions. Use the email address you gave us so we can find your records. See the privacy policy.

Copyright notices

Choose "Copyright notice" and include the details listed in our notice-and-takedown procedure, so we can act quickly.

Press

Journalists can ask about our guides, the Down.Town Index or our contests. Tell us your publication and deadline.

Contests

Questions about Downtown Lens or the Best Block Awards? Read the official rules first; most answers are there.

Accessibility

Found a barrier? Tell us the page, what you were trying to do and the device or assistive technology you use. See our accessibility statement.
