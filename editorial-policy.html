Editorial policy

Research and sources

Guides are built from primary sources wherever possible: city and transit agencies, tourism boards, official event organisers, institutions and published data. We visit, walk and ride where we can, and we note when information is likely to change.

Use of technology

We use software tools — including AI writing and research assistance — to help draft, organise and check content. Every published guide is reviewed by an editor, and factual claims are checked against sources before publication. Tools never decide Index scores.

Scores and rankings

The Down.Town Index is an editorial judgement made with a published method. Scores cannot be bought or influenced by advertisers or sponsors.

Updates

Each guide shows the month it was last reviewed. We review every guide at least once a year and sooner when something important changes.

Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it quickly. Significant corrections are noted on the page. Report an error.

Advertising and sponsorship

Ads, sponsored placements, Featured and Spotlight listings and partner modules are labelled. Sponsors may suggest topics but never review or approve editorial content.

Referral partners

When readers ask for introductions, we choose partners based on fit and service quality. Partner fees do not influence our guides.
